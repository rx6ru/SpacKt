## Verified behaviour

- Decimal prices and quantities convert directly to scaled integers.
- Seed, epoch, and configuration reproduce the event sequence.
- A generated trade consumes available quantity at the previous best opposite level.
- Public books contain 20–50 sorted positive levels per side and remain uncrossed.
- Book mutation sequences are contiguous. Snapshot copies do not expose mutable state.
- Low and high simulator configurations keep prices inside the operational range.
- Every candle interval folds the same trades.
- Hand-calculated OHLCV cases pass for real trades and empty carry-forward buckets.
- Circular retention returns copied ascending history after wrap-around.

## Review corrections

The corrected refill selects an unused, valid price and preserves required depth.
Simulator invariant failures stop immediately instead of silently producing incomplete events.
Candle retention now uses a circular buffer, as planned.
